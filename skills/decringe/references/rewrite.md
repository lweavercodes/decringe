# Rewrite

Read the selected module guidance and [rules](rules.md). Identify what already works and the constraints the user wants preserved. Keep the requested scope: a hero rewrite does not authorize changing the product's positioning throughout the site.

Review substance before phrasing. Repair mismatches among audience, product facts, claims, and action first. A sentence that sounds more human can still be misleading. Translate only what can be supported; do not replace infrastructure terms with invented time savings or business outcomes.

Rewrite at the smallest scale that solves the problem. Preserve meaning, useful specifics, terms of sale, quotes, deliberate voice, accessibility labels, and strings that are functional identifiers. If a meaningful tradeoff is uncertain, offer a conservative version and state the assumption outside the copy.

If editing UI files, use the requested app's existing copy/i18n structure. Inspect the string's use, interpolation variables, pluralization, and actual handler/state. Do not rename keys, routes, backend fields, or change product behavior to make a proposed label true. Report behavior mismatches separately.

Reread the finished surface and compare its factual claims with the original and context. Scan substantial drafts again once. Deliver changed paths or the replacement text, then briefly explain consequential changes and remaining assumptions.

Follow [reviewer](reviewer.md) for the explicit delegated semantic pass, correction and recheck. The local scan does not replace it. For an audit, stop at findings without editing.
