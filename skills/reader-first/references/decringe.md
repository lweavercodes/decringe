# Decringe

Remove synthetic rhetoric without replacing the author's meaning or voice. Read the style rules in [rules](rules.md). The scanner offers candidate locations, not a mandatory deletion list.

Watch for inflated significance, empty praise, recurring canned contrasts, vague nouns, theatrical fragments, repeated three-part lists, and conclusions that merely restate the preceding paragraph. Replace padding with the actual fact or remove it when nothing is lost.

Keep intentional repetition, humor, useful comparisons, rhythmic lists, contractions, and technical precision when they serve this reader. Em dashes, “crucial,” “robust,” and “seamless” are not intrinsically wrong. “Seamless copper pipe” is not vague SaaS reassurance.

Preserve facts and certainty. Do not fix “saves time” by inventing “saves three hours.” Do not give a mildly awkward draft a more persuasive but unsupported claim. If a phrase conceals a substance problem, flag it and repair only within the supplied facts.

Do a complete reread after changes. Return edited copy with a short note for consequential omissions or unresolved facts. Do not label the author as AI, promise detector evasion, or run endless passes to eliminate all candidates.
