# Audience — fictional example

## Primary reader

Independent language teachers, evaluating a worksheet-draft tool after a lesson. They have lesson notes and want to prepare practice for their students. They know “worksheet,” “draft,” “assign,” and “student”; backend architecture is not a buying criterion for this example.

These are scenario assumptions for a demonstration, not customer research. No quotations or measured demand are available.

## Decision criteria

Teachers need to know what goes in, whether they can edit the result, how it reaches students, and what the trial permits. They retain responsibility for reviewing generated answers.
